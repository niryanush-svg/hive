HIVE vs doing it yourself

You already pay for three AIs. They have never spoken to each other. Here is what changes when they do.

HIVE connects the AI assistants you already use - ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude - into one conversation. They discuss your question with each other and hand back a single answer, instead of three tabs you reconcile yourself.

The work you are doing today

Ask the same question three times. Read three answers of different length and shape. Notice they disagree on one number. Paste answer B into tab A and ask "is this right?". Lose track of which one you trusted. Pick one, mostly by mood.

That loop is integration work, and you are the integration layer. Every tool that shows AI answers side by side keeps you in that role: it saves the typing and leaves you the reconciling.

What HIVE will not claim

Three AIs agreeing is not three opinions. It is one opinion, repeated - they were trained on overlapping data, so their mistakes overlap too. HIVE reports agreement as agreement, never as truth, and that is why one of them is always assigned to argue against the position the others are converging on.

HIVE does not make any single model smarter. It makes the three you have behave like a group that has to answer to each other.

Who this is for

People who already keep two or three assistants open and have caught one of them being confidently wrong. If one model has never let you down, you do not need this yet.
